# WhiteBoard — Master Build Prompt for AI Coding Agents

## A Technical, Product & Orchestration Specification for Building an Inclusive Digital Learning Ecosystem

---

# Brand Identity

**Product Name:** WhiteBoard

**Motto:** **Ask. Learn. Become.**

**Brand Inspiration:**
Inspired by the Socratic tradition of questioning, the pursuit of knowledge and the belief that education enables human potential.

**Brand Philosophy:**
Every great idea begins on a blank white board.

WhiteBoard is designed as a place where learners ask questions, educators share knowledge, institutions extend their reach and opportunities are created for people who may otherwise be excluded from quality education.

---

# 1. Project Vision

## 1.1 Product Overview

**WhiteBoard** is an inclusive digital learning ecosystem designed to connect learners, parents, educators, tutors, schools, universities, training institutions, scholarship providers and education partners.

The platform combines:

* Structured digital learning
* Multi-curriculum education
* AI-powered learning support
* Institutional learning partnerships
* Homeschool and family learning
* Tutor and educator participation
* Scholarship discovery and administration
* Learning progress tracking
* Virtual and hybrid classes
* Inclusive access initiatives
* Gender equity programs supporting access to education

WhiteBoard should not be designed merely as another Learning Management System.

Its long-term vision is to become a **digital education ecosystem and opportunity platform**.

---

## 1.2 Core Mission

To make quality education, learning opportunities and academic support more accessible, inclusive and personalized regardless of gender, background, geography or economic circumstances.

---

## 1.3 Core Value Proposition

WhiteBoard brings together three traditionally disconnected areas:

1. **Learning**
2. **Institutions**
3. **Educational Opportunity**

A learner should be able to:

* Learn
* Practice
* Track progress
* Connect with educators
* Participate in institutional programs
* Discover scholarships
* Apply for educational opportunities
* Access mentorship and support

All within one ecosystem.

WhiteBoard must support a flexible, role-based multi-user ecosystem.

Learners are the central users of the platform.

They may include:

* School students
* Homeschool learners
* University students
* College students
* Technical and vocational learners
* Adult learners
* Independent learners

* Grants
* Sponsorships
* Fellowships
* Educational competitions

* Country

1. Scholarship discovery
2. Eligibility review
3. Application requirements
4. Document submission

6. Application tracking

* Draft
* Submitted
* Under Review
* Additional Information Required
* Shortlisted
* Approved
* Declined
* Awarded

---

WhiteBoard may eventually develop an optional **Educational Opportunity Profile**.

This should never automatically determine scholarship eligibility.

Instead, it may help learners discover potentially relevant opportunities using information such as:

* Academic performance
* Learning consistency
* Subject interests
* Skills
* Achievements
* Career aspirations
* Self-declared financial need where appropriate

All matching must remain transparent and should not discriminate unfairly.

# 13. Privacy & Child Protection

Because WhiteBoard processes learner and potentially children's educational information:

* Privacy must be a foundational architecture requirement.
* Parent or guardian consent must be implemented where required.
* Child data must never be exposed across accounts.
* Institutions must only access authorized learner information.
* Scholarship providers should only access information submitted by applicants or explicitly authorized by users.
* Data deletion workflows must be available.
* No behavioural advertising.
* No sale of learner data.
* No third-party advertising pixels.

The system should use Kenya's Data Protection Act as a baseline and be designed with strong international privacy principles.

# 14. Accessibility & Low-Bandwidth Design

WhiteBoard should be designed for broad accessibility.

Requirements:

* Mobile-first
* Low-bandwidth-first
* Progressive Web App support
* Optimized media
* Offline support where practical
* WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
* Clear typography
* Keyboard accessibility
* Responsive design

The platform should perform well on mid-range Android devices and unreliable internet connections.

# 18. Mandatory Engineering Guardrails

All agents must:

* Never hardcode secrets.
* Validate all input server-side.
* Enforce authorization server-side.
* Write tests for new functionality.
* Avoid destructive migrations without review.
* Minimize dependencies.
* Optimize for low infrastructure cost.
* Maintain documentation.
* Use feature flags for experimental functionality.
* Protect children's and learners' information.
* Avoid discriminatory AI behavior.
* Ensure scholarship matching remains transparent.

# 20. Final Product Instruction to AI Coding Agents

Build WhiteBoard as a scalable, inclusive education ecosystem rather than a narrow online learning application.

Every architectural decision should support the long-term ability to connect:

> **Learners → Educators → Families → Institutions → Opportunities**

The MVP must remain disciplined and lightweight.

Do not attempt to build every future feature immediately.

Build strong foundations first.

Prioritize:

1. Learner value
2. Educational access
3. Institutional trust
4. Privacy
5. Inclusion
6. Low-bandwidth accessibility
7. Sustainable operating costs
8. Scalable architecture

The ultimate objective is for WhiteBoard to become a trusted platform where learning, opportunity and educational institutions meet.
